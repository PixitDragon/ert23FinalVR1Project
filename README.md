Welcome to:
## Spider Solutions ##



What's your project all about?

> My project is a puzzle game where you have to put different color/patterend runes in order on pedestals to complete the ultimate goal: find a bug for your spider friend Potato Jeremy to eat. 



What was the additional skill you worked on?

> I wanted to work on my 3D modeling skills, so I made the shape of the room in Blender. I didn't know what I was doing, so it was non-manifold and had all sorts of strange errors. I get help from a frien who knows how to use Blender, and he helped teach me how to do it better. When I went back to Blender and made the puzzle runes, I had way fewer errors and I was able to make custom textures to go on them! There's still some issues, but it's much better than my first attempt! (I also made the big paintings in the second room, but that was something I already knew how to do)


What was a key challenge you overcame or an element of the project you're particularly proud of/want to highlight.

> A hard part was figuring out how to design a puzzles that weren't too hard for players to understand, but not so simple that I was giving them the answers. Part of my solution to this was coloring the runes instead of just having them differ in patterns. This change also allowed me to create new puzzle types, such as the letters in Potato Jeremy's name being highlighted to show the solution to the first puzzle.
